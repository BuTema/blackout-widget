# Google Play — English listing

**Title (max 30):** Power Schedules (Ukraine)

**Short description (max 80):**
Outage schedule on your home screen: widget, alerts, several addresses

**Full description:**
Power Schedules shows when the lights are on and when they will go off, right on your home screen.

• A widget that adapts to its size, from a narrow column to a large card: the current period, the next ones and a 48-pill day graph.
• Several addresses: home, parents, a country house. Drag the cards to reorder them.
• Find your queue by address with suggestions, or pick it by hand.
• Today and tomorrow, with a switch right in the widget.
• Emergency outages are highlighted in red.
• Notifications when the schedule changes or tomorrow's appears, and reminders before power goes off or comes back.
• Light and dark themes, adjustable widget transparency.
• No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your device.

Supported networks: DTEK Odesa, Kyiv Oblast, Dnipro and Kyiv.

The app is unofficial and not affiliated with DTEK. Schedules come from the public outage pages of the operators, who are responsible for their accuracy. Open source: github.com/BuTema/blackout-widget
